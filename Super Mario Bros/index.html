RETRO PLATFORM
ADVENTURE

An original 8-bit inspired browser platformer

Works with keyboard or touch controls

HOW TO PLAY

Move: ← / → or A / D

Jump: Space / W / ↑

Pause: P

Restart: R

Collect coins, stomp enemies, grab power-ups and reach the flag.

Three levels get progressively harder. You have 3 lives.

PAUSED

Press P or tap Resume to continue.
